# Extreme Note — Support

Got a bug, question, or feature request? Reach out:

## 📧 Email
**Extreme_Note@proton.me**
Include in your email:
- Chrome version (`chrome://version`)
- A short description of what you tried and what happened
- Screenshot or DevTools console output if relevant

## 🐛 What to expect
- Bug reports: replied within 2-3 business days
- Feature requests: collected and prioritized for next minor version
- Security issues: please email directly (not in any public forum) — see the [Privacy Policy](./)

## 🔐 Account / Data issues
- Notes are stored locally in `chrome.storage.local`. To wipe everything: `chrome://extensions` → Extreme Note → Remove
- Google Drive files created by the extension live in your own Drive — delete them there directly
- The extension does not have access to anything else in your account

## ⏱️ Limits to be aware of
- Up to 5 open tabs at once
- Up to 50 saved notes in the local library
- Local storage cap: ~10 MB total
- Translation (when MyMemory fallback is used): ~1000 requests/day, 500 chars per request
